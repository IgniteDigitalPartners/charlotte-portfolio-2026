# Charlotte Smith Portfolio 2026 — Design Brainstorm

## Response A — Editorial Broadsheet
**Probability: 0.07**

**Design Movement:** Swiss International Typographic Style meets contemporary editorial broadsheet

**Core Principles:**
- Strict typographic hierarchy as the primary visual language
- Asymmetric column grids that break predictably
- Ink-black on cream with a single copper accent
- Every element earns its place; nothing decorative

**Color Philosophy:** Obsidian (#0A0A0A), Warm Cream (#F5F0E8), Copper (#C87941). The palette signals authority and permanence — like a well-designed annual report, not a startup landing page.

**Layout Paradigm:** Newspaper-inspired multi-column grid. Sections break into 2-3-4 column configurations. Hero is full-bleed text only, no imagery. Work section uses a masonry-style card grid with large case study numbers.

**Signature Elements:**
- Oversized section numerals (01, 02, 03) as structural dividers
- Thin copper rule lines as section separators
- Pull-quote blocks in italic serif

**Interaction Philosophy:** Minimal animation. Hover states reveal copper underlines. Scroll-triggered fade-ins at 200ms.

**Animation:** Subtle opacity transitions on scroll. No parallax. No transforms beyond scale(0.97) on buttons.

**Typography System:** Playfair Display (display headlines) + DM Sans (body, labels). Headline weight 700, body weight 400/500.

## Response B — Cinematic Dark Luxury
**Probability: 0.08**

**Design Movement:** High-end consultancy meets cinematic brand identity — think McKinsey meets Pentagram

**Core Principles:**
- Dark obsidian backgrounds with copper and cream typography
- Generous whitespace as a luxury signal
- Case study imagery presented as full-bleed cinematic panels
- Credential section styled like a printed CV with precision

**Color Philosophy:** Deep obsidian (#0D0D0D) as the dominant surface. Copper (#C87941) for accents and highlights. Cream (#F2EFEA) for body text on dark. The palette is Charlotte's own IDP brand, applied to the portfolio itself — total coherence.

**Layout Paradigm:** Full-width sections that alternate between dark and cream. Hero is split: large typographic statement left, real client screenshot right. Work grid is horizontal scroll on mobile, 2-column on desktop with hover-reveal overlays.

**Signature Elements:**
- Case study cards with real screenshots as backgrounds, text overlay on hover
- Thin horizontal rules in copper between sections
- Roman numeral section markers (I, II, III, IV)

**Interaction Philosophy:** Hover states lift cards with subtle shadow. Screenshots zoom slightly on hover (scale 1.03). Smooth scroll with section snap.

**Animation:** Entrance animations using framer-motion. Cards stagger in at 60ms intervals. Section headings slide up 20px on scroll entry.

**Typography System:** Cormorant Garamond (display, italic accents) + Outfit (body, labels, nav). Strong contrast between serif display and geometric sans.

## Response C — Structured Modernist
**Probability: 0.06**

**Design Movement:** Structured modernism — clean, confident, built for senior hiring managers who scan fast

**Core Principles:**
- Light cream background, maximum readability
- Bold typographic hierarchy with generous line-height
- Real client screenshots as primary visual proof
- CV and portfolio unified in one linear scroll

**Color Philosophy:** Warm white (#FAF8F4), Ink navy (#0F1B2D), Copper accent (#C87941). Professional, readable, and differentiated from the typical "white and blue" portfolio.

**Layout Paradigm:** Single-column editorial with intentional left-offset text blocks. Stats bar spans full width. Work section alternates left/right image-text pairs. Timeline CV uses a vertical rule with dot markers.

**Signature Elements:**
- Left-aligned section labels in small caps copper
- Alternating image-text layout for case studies
- Sticky left-side progress indicator on desktop

**Interaction Philosophy:** Clean hover states. No gimmicks. Smooth scroll. Mobile-first.

**Animation:** Fade-up on scroll entry. 150ms transitions. Reduced motion respected.

**Typography System:** Fraunces (display, variable weight) + Inter (body). Fraunces gives editorial warmth; Inter ensures ATS-friendly readability.

---

## Selected Approach: Response B — Cinematic Dark Luxury

Charlotte's own brand is obsidian and copper. The portfolio should wear the same clothes as the work it showcases. The dark luxury approach signals seniority, coherence, and commercial discipline — exactly the message a senior marketing strategist needs to send to potential employers. Real client screenshots displayed as cinematic panels prove the work without needing to explain it.
